    How to play & scoring

    Click dice to select a scoring combination. Keep & roll adds its points to your turn and rolls the remaining dice. Bank & pass adds the selected points and your turn score to your total.

    A roll with no scoring combination is a Farkle: all unbanked turn points are lost. Use all remaining dice to get hot dice and roll six fresh dice. Dice from separate rolls cannot be combined. Every selected die must score.
